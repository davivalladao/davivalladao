# Davi Valladão

Associate Professor of Industrial Engineering at **PUC-Rio**, Brazil, and co-founder and research director of **LAMPS**.

I work on optimization under uncertainty, risk analysis, and machine learning, with applications in energy and finance. My research connects mathematical models with computational tools for forecasting and decision-making.

## Research software

Selected open-source projects developed with students and collaborators at [LAMPS/PUC-Rio](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC):

| Project | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [PolieDRO.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/PolieDRO.jl) | Distributionally robust classification and regression |
| [StateSpaceLearning.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/StateSpaceLearning.jl) | Time-series analysis and forecasting with state space learning |
| [Sarimax.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/Sarimax.jl) | SARIMA/SARIMAX estimation and forecasting in Julia |
| [UnobservedComponentsGAS](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/UnobservedComponentsGAS) | Score-driven unobserved components models |
| [HydroPowerModels.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/HydroPowerModels.jl) | Multistage stochastic optimization for hydrothermal power systems |
