# Davi Valladão

Associate Professor of Industrial Engineering at **PUC-Rio**, Brazil, and co-founder and research director of the **LAMPS research laboratory at PUC-Rio**.

I work on optimization under uncertainty, forecasting, and computational methods for decision-making in energy and finance. My research combines stochastic and robust optimization, risk modeling, and machine learning.

## Research software

Research software developed by students under my academic supervision, in collaboration with researchers at [LAMPS/PUC-Rio](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC). My role in these projects is academic supervision; the software was implemented by the students.

**Start here:** [PolieDRO.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/PolieDRO.jl) for distributionally robust learning, [StateSpaceLearning.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/StateSpaceLearning.jl) for forecasting, and [HydroPowerModels.jl](https://github.com/LAMPSPUC/HydroPowerModels.jl) for hydrothermal power systems.
